ABOUT US

Govt. Hr. Sec. School, Alagankulam has grown into one of the region's most respected institutions. We combine rigorous academics with holistic development, nurturing every student's potential for the challenges of the modern world.

Our philosophy centres on experiential learning, critical thinking and character building. We don't just teach subjects — we inspire a lifelong love of learning.
