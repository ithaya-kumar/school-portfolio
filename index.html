About Our School

Est. 2001

Govt. Hr. Sec. School, Alagankulam is a premier Tamil Nadu Government educational institution offering quality education to students from all communities.

We offer comprehensive education from Class VI to XII with Science and Arts streams, guided by the Tamil Nadu State Board syllabus. Our dedicated faculty combines academic rigour with holistic character development.

🎯

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence that empowers students to become responsible, knowledgeable and productive citizens.

📘

Our Mission

Providing quality, value-based education to nurture well-rounded individuals ready for the modern world.

Government Welfare Schemes

The Tamil Nadu Government provides numerous welfare schemes ensuring every student gets the best education without financial barriers.

📚

Free Textbooks

Free textbooks provided to all students from Class I to XII every academic year under the Tamil Nadu Government scheme.

👕

Free Uniform

Two sets of school uniforms, shoes and socks provided free to all enrolled students annually.

🍱

Noon Meal Programme

Nutritious mid-day meal provided to all students on every working day to support health and encourage attendance.

💰

Educational Assistance

Scholarships, stipends and financial support for students from economically weaker sections (EWS) of society.

💻

Digital Learning

Smart classrooms and digital tablets provided under the Ennum Ezhuthum and other state digital literacy schemes.

🚌

Free Bus Pass

Government school students are entitled to free bus passes for daily travel to and from school under state transport schemes.
